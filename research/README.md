# research/ — historical design drafts

Everything in this folder is a **working draft** produced while designing DMF Mentor
(the support/mentor agent) and its scam-detection layer. These files are kept for
provenance, not as protocol truth.

Rules for agents:

- For anything factual about dmfUSD (addresses, fees, backing, tests, security) use
  `overview/`, `protocol/`, `security/` and `reference/`. Those are maintained.
- Numbers in these drafts — chain counts, fee splits, roadmap phases — may predate the
  current deployment and MUST NOT be quoted as current facts.
- Nothing here is served on dmfam.org and nothing here should be treated as a promise
  of shipped functionality.
